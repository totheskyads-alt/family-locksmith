When a lock fails at a business, every minute can cost money. We work with offices, shops, warehouses, schools and managing agents to keep premises secure, compliant and running, with priority response for lockouts and faults.

How we help

We design and install master-key and keyed-alike systems so the right people access the right doors with fewer keys, fit access control and high-security locks, and handle everything from a single cylinder change to a multi-site rollout. All work is carried out to commercial and, where relevant, fire-safety standards.
