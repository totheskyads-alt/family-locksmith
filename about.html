Family Locksmith started for the same reason many people call us: someone we cared about was locked out, charged far too much, and treated badly by a locksmith who saw an emergency as an opportunity. We thought it should be done properly, so we built a company around it.

Today we're a nationwide network of local, vetted engineers covering all of England, 24 hours a day. But the values are still the ones we started with: turn up fast, be honest about the price, do the job right, and treat every home like it's our own.

What travels with us

Half of what makes a locksmith slow is driving off to a supplier mid-job. The van is stocked so that the common failures get finished the same visit.

Euro cylinders, every common size

Offsets vary door to door, so we carry the range rather than measuring and coming back tomorrow.

Multipoint gearboxes

The part that actually fails on uPVC and composite doors. Replacing it costs a fraction of a new door.

Anti-snap and British Standard

TS007 three star cylinders and BS3621 mortice locks, for the specification your insurer asks for.
