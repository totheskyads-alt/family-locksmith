uPVC and composite doors are on most modern homes, and their multi-point locking mechanisms are the single most common lock problem we're called out to. The good news: it's almost always fixable, and rarely means a whole new door.

How we fix a door that will not lock

We find the actual fault first. It is usually a worn gearbox, a failed cylinder or a door that has dropped out of alignment. We carry the common multi-point mechanisms and euro cylinders to fix most doors on the first visit. If your door has dropped or stiffened over time, we'll also adjust the alignment so it closes smoothly again.

Where a lock has failed completely and you're locked out, we can gain entry with minimal disruption and fit a new mechanism on the spot.

What your door is telling you

What you noticeWhat it usually is
Handle lifts, key will not turnWorn gearbox inside the multi-point mechanism
Handle flops or spins freelySnapped spindle or a failed gearbox cam
Only locks if you lift and shoveThe door has dropped and needs realigning on its hinges
Key turns but nothing engagesCylinder cam broken, or the cylinder has come loose
Key will not go in at allSeized or snapped cylinder, often after cold weather
