Managing rental property means locks change hands constantly. Between every tenancy the property should be re-secured, and when something fails, you need someone dependable who turns up. That's exactly what we do.

How we work with landlords

We offer fast turnaround lock changes between tenancies so a new tenant gets fresh, secure locks and old keys stop working. We can set up keyed-alike suites across a portfolio to cut down on key management, ensure locks meet British Standard and insurance requirements, and provide clear invoicing for your records.

Prefer a single point of contact and priority booking? We're happy to work as your go-to locksmith across all your properties.
