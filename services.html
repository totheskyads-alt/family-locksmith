What these services look like in practice

The service list above is the menu. This is what the visit actually involves once we are at your door.

Emergency lockout

The door shut behind you

Keys on the kitchen table and it is half eleven at night. We open the great majority of doors without drilling, so you keep the lock you already had and pay for the callout, not a new cylinder.

With you in around 25 minutes
uPVC and composite

The handle lifts but the key will not turn

That is nearly always the multipoint gearbox rather than the door itself. We carry the mechanisms that fail most often, so it is usually sorted in one visit instead of two.

Most repairs done the same day
Burglary repair

Someone has been through the door

We make the property secure first, board up if the frame has gone, and book the permanent repair once you have spoken to your insurer. You are not left waiting overnight with a door that will not shut.

Same evening, seven days a week
Moving in

You do not know who still has a key

Previous owners, their family, a builder, whoever held the spare. Changing the cylinders on the external doors settles the question on your first day rather than your first scare.

Usually under an hour
Insurance work

Your policy asks for anti-snap locks

Normally BS3621 or TS007 three star, depending on how the wording runs. We fit to the specification and confirm what went in, in writing, so you can forward it straight to the insurer.

Documented for your insurer
Landlords and agents

New tenant moving in on Monday

Cylinder changes between tenancies, keyed alike where you want one key per property, and invoiced to the agency rather than the tenant. We work around the changeover date.

Booked around your changeover
Snapped key

Half the key is still in the lock

Do not push the other half back in, that is what turns a ten minute job into a new lock. We extract the broken section and, if the lock came through it, cut you a fresh set from what is left.

Extracted without drilling, usually
Shops and offices

The shutter lock has gone and you cannot open up

Aluminium doors, roller shutters and master suited systems where one key needs to open several doors. We know what closing for the morning costs you, so commercial calls go to the front.

Trading again the same morning
