Emergency lockout locksmith

Locked out? Stay put. A local, insured locksmith can be with you in around 25 minutes, day or night, and open the door without damaging it.

Being locked out is stressful, especially late at night or with children waiting. It happens to everyone, a snapped key, a door slammed on the latch, or keys left inside. Whatever's happened, we're on call 24 hours a day to get you back in quickly and safely.

How we get you back in

Our engineers are trained in non-destructive entry, which means we pick, bypass or manipulate the lock open wherever possible, so you're not left with a broken door or an expensive replacement. In most cases you'll be back inside within minutes of us arriving, and your existing lock will keep working exactly as before.

Before we set off, we'll confirm a clear, fixed price, there's never a call-out charge and never a surprise added at the end.

What happens after you call

  1. The call
    We ask three things

    Where you are, what kind of door it is, and whether anyone is shut inside. That is enough to quote you a firm price.

  2. ~25 min
    An engineer arrives

    The nearest available locksmith comes to you with the tools and stock for your type of lock.

  3. Minutes
    The door opens

    Picked or bypassed wherever possible, so your lock keeps working and nothing needs replacing.

  4. Before we go
    You check it yourself

    We wait while you lock and unlock it. Then you pay the price we agreed on the phone, nothing added.
