Lock changes & repairs

Moved in, lost a set of keys, or simply want the locks to be yours alone. We fit locks to British Standard and insurance requirements, at a price agreed upfront.

Call now WhatsApp us

There are lots of good reasons to change a lock: you've moved into a new home and don't know who else has a key, you've lost a set, a relationship has ended, or a lock is simply worn out and sticking. Whatever the reason, we'll fit the right lock for your door and your insurer.

Choosing the right lock for your door

We supply and fit trusted brands, Yale, Union, Chubb, Avocet and ERA, and can match locks to the British Standard (BS3621) that most home insurance policies require. If a lock can be repaired rather than replaced, we'll tell you honestly and save you money.

Moved house? Ask about re-keying or changing all the barrels so old keys no longer work, a fast, cost-effective way to secure a new home.
